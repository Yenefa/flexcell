## Boundaries that must travel with these numbers

- #77 and #81 are two different kinds of evidence: #77 recomputes the archived window `40916246921`, while #81 adds one new declared local window at `19caa019`. Presenting the #77 replay as an execution of the new runtime would be false. See [PREPOSITION_WORLD_FRAME_20261031.md](PREPOSITION_WORLD_FRAME_20261031.md) and [#81](https://github.com/Yenefa/flexcell/issues/81).
- #78 qualifies the fault-response chain at the guard input boundary (`boundary: guard_input_pose`), not the physics of a slipping grasp: a rigid `DetachableJoint` either holds or disappears and cannot slip, so no physical slip was produced or claimed. One task, one injection, no rate and no MTBF. See [FALSE_ATTACH_INJECTION_20261031.md](FALSE_ATTACH_INJECTION_20261031.md).
- #79 calibrates one known direction only: gravity loading is signed and settled, and the fixture is a resting box with no arm, no attachment and no grasp. Lateral, oblique, tensile and torque cases are not calibrated, and collisions without a contact sensor stay UNKNOWN. See [SIGNED_CONTACT_FORCE_20261031.md](SIGNED_CONTACT_FORCE_20261031.md).
- #80 is an MTC skill backend compared across three carry backends. #86 added the real legacy-by-teleport and legacy-by-joint windows on the local stack, but #59's CI execution condition is still unmet, so nothing here closes an admission issue. See [#80](https://github.com/Yenefa/flexcell/issues/80) and [#86](https://github.com/Yenefa/flexcell/issues/86).
- Two different states must not be merged: the historical native-five-cycle stream-stale failure (run `41116653853` at `c2583ae`, 787.690270 ms stale, consumer-pause source UNKNOWN, mechanism reproduced only in a virtual-clock experiment) and the Actions jobs that never started (runs `41202950948`/`41202950949` at `9a923e7` with 0 steps, annotation naming failed payments or a spending limit). Only the existing logs and receipts are quoted, and no root cause is guessed for either. See [NATIVE_AUDIT_LIFETIME_20261031.md](NATIVE_AUDIT_LIFETIME_20261031.md) and [NATIVE_CYCLE_DEADLINE_20261103.md](NATIVE_CYCLE_DEADLINE_20261103.md).
- The missing-assertion observation in the standalone probe stands: it holds five assertions plus one observation, and exit 0 means the probe's own assertions held, not that a criterion was admitted. See [BACKEND_ADMISSION_20261027.md](BACKEND_ADMISSION_20261027.md).
